1.3Core Concept

MR 與 VR 的差異How Mixed Reality differs from Virtual Reality

本節從四個面向拆解，這四個面向也是你未來寫技術文件、做畢專簡報時該有的分析架構。 This section breaks the question down along four dimensions — the same analytical frame you should use later when writing technical documents or presenting your capstone.

一句話先記住：VR 問的是「我要造一個什麼世界」；MR 問的是「這個世界原本長什麼樣，我要往裡面加什麼」。 One line to remember: VR asks "what world shall I build?" MR asks "what does this world already look like, and what am I adding to it?"
1.3.1Definitions1 / 2

名稱定義：一條光譜，四個名詞One continuum, four terms

Milgram 與 Kishino（1994）提出「真實—虛擬連續體」。所有名詞都是這條線上的位置，不是彼此對立的技術。 Milgram and Kishino (1994) proposed the Reality–Virtuality Continuum. Every one of these terms is a position on that line, not a technology opposed to the others.

圖 1.3.1｜XR 是集合名詞；AR 與 MR 的分界點在於「虛擬物件是否取得並使用真實世界的幾何資料」。 Fig. 1.3.1｜XR is a collective noun. The line between AR and MR is whether the virtual object obtains and uses real-world geometry.

常見誤用：把「有彩色透視畫面」就叫做 MR。只有透視、虛擬物件卻懸空穿牆的，本質上仍是 AR。有沒有空間幾何，才是 MR 的分水嶺。 A common misuse: calling anything with colour passthrough "MR". If the passthrough is there but virtual objects float and clip through walls, it is still AR. Spatial geometry is the dividing line.
1.3.2Technical1 / 2

技術面：多了三條資料流Technically, MR adds three data streams

VR 的畫面只有一個來源：Unity 渲染結果。MR 的畫面是「真實影像 ＋ 空間資料 ＋ 虛擬渲染」三者合成。 A VR frame has a single source: Unity's render. An MR frame is a composite of real imagery, spatial data and the virtual render.

1.3.3Experience2 / 2

兩種體驗的特徵Characteristics of each experience

VR

執行效果特徵What VR gives you

  • 沉浸感高，世界觀完全可控High immersion; total control over the world you present
  • 場景可任意大小，不受房間限制Scene size is unlimited — the real room does not constrain it
  • 需護欄（Guardian）防撞，活動範圍受限A Guardian boundary is needed; physical movement is restricted
  • 移動方式易造成暈動症Artificial locomotion readily causes motion sickness
  • 多人同場＝共享同一個虛擬場景Multiplayer means everyone shares one virtual scene
MR

執行效果特徵What MR gives you

  • 安全感高，看得到家具與同學，可站著走動Safer — you can see furniture and classmates, and walk around
  • 場景大小＝你的房間大小，每個玩家的關卡都不同The level is the size of your room — every player's level is different
  • 暈動症大幅降低（視覺與體感一致）Motion sickness drops sharply; vision and proprioception agree
  • 虛擬物件對齊失準會立刻被察覺Any misalignment — clipping, floating — is instantly noticed
  • 受真實光線、材質、掃描品質影響，可控性低Real lighting, materials and scan quality all intrude; less controllable
教室實務提醒：MR 的關卡是「掃描出來的」。EB101 每一台頭盔都要各自做一次 Space Setup，而且桌椅移動後掃描資料就會失準。第 3 週會預留掃描時間。 Classroom reality: an MR level is scanned, not authored. Every headset in EB101 must run its own Space Setup, and the scan goes stale as soon as the desks are moved. Week 3 reserves lab time for this.

1.5Projects1 / 2

製作主題一：MR 漆彈射擊Theme 1 — MR Paintball Shooting

兩個主題不是各自獨立的練習——主題二會重用主題一的貼圖與物件池系統，這個「重構成可重用元件」的過程本身就是評分項目。 The two themes are not separate exercises. Theme 2 reuses the decal and pooling systems from Theme 1, and that refactoring into reusable components is itself part of the assessment.

主題一 · W4–W9
  • MRUK 房間射線 → 取得 hit.point 與 hit.normal Raycast against the MRUK room to obtain the hit point and its surface normal.
  • 法向量對齊貼片，沿法線外推 5mm 避免 Z-fighting Align the decal to that normal and offset it 5 mm along it to avoid Z-fighting.
  • Object Pool 管理大量漆點，避免 GC 尖峰造成掉幀 Pool the splats so garbage-collection spikes never drop a frame.
